Siya,

Tumhari tareef karna mere liye aasan bhi hai aur mushkil bhi. Aasan isliye ki tumhare mein itni achhi baatein hain ki alfaaz khud aa jaate hain, aur mushkil isliye ki jitna likhta hoon, lagta hai ki abhi bahut kuch baaki hai.

Tumhari sundarta sirf chehre ki nahi hai. Tumhara andaaz, tumhari baat karne ki tameez, tumhara chalna, tumhara hansna, sab mein ek alag si garima hai. Tum jis jagah hoti ho, wahan ka mahaul apne aap narm ho jaata hai.

Tumhari aankhein mujhe bahut pasand hain. Unmein ek gehraai hai, ek apnapan hai. Tum kuch na bolo tab bhi bahut kuch keh deti ho.

Tumhara dil bahut saaf hai. Tum logon ki parwah karti ho, chhoti chhoti baaton ka dhyaan rakhti ho, aur dikhawe ke bina apnapan nibhati ho. Aajkal aise log bahut kam milte hain, aur tum unmein se sabse khaas ho.

Tumhari saadgi mujhe sabse zyada pasand hai. Tumhe kisi dikhawe ki zaroorat nahi, tum jaisi ho waisi hi sabse pyaari ho. Tumhe dekhkar ye yaqeen aata hai ki asli khoobsurti dikhawe mein nahi, sachchai mein hoti hai.

Tum mehnat karna jaanti ho, aur himmat bhi rakhti ho. Kai baar tum bina bataye bahut kuch sambhal leti ho. Mujhe tumpe garv hota hai, aur main chahta hoon ki tum khud bhi apne aap par garv karo.

Tumhari baaton mein ek alag sukoon hai. Tumse baat karke lagta hai jaise koi apna sun raha hai, bina judge kiye, bina jaldi kiye. Tumhari awaaz mere liye sabse sukoon dene wali awaaz hai.

Tumhari hansi mein ek jaadu hai. Jab tum dil se hansti ho to lagta hai ki poori duniya thodi der ke liye ruk gayi ho. Kaash main har roz wo hansi dekh sakun.

Tum bahut samajhdaar ho, bahut sweet ho, thodi si shararati bhi ho, aur yahi sab milkar tumhe tum banata hai. Mujhe tumhari har ek aadat pasand hai, chahe wo badi ho ya chhoti.

Tum us phool ki tarah ho jo shor nahi machata, par jahan hota hai wahan ki jagah khushboo se bhar deta hai. Tum meri zindagi ka wo hissa ban chuki ho jiske bina sab kuch adhura lagta hai.

Main ye sab isliye likh raha hoon kyunki tum ye sunne ki haqdaar ho. Tum bahut khaas ho, bahut pyaari ho, aur bahut kuch achha deserve karti ho. Ye baat kabhi mat bhoolna.

Aur haan, ye sab to sirf shuruaat hai. Agle section mein 100 wajah hain, aur woh bhi poori nahi hain. 💗

— Tumhara Shourya
